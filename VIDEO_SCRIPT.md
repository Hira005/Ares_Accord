# Demo video script (target 4 minutes, screen recording + voice)

**0:00–0:25 – Intro.** "ARES ACCORD: five agents negotiate a shared pool. The validator is outside the language model and
the Commander cannot approve without PASS and four matching votes." Show the architecture diagram.

**0:25–0:50 – Start.** Show Judge Controls: resource pool 79/52/59/26/17, press **Start scenario**. Point at the model name
in the header (or the FALLBACK banner if no key).

**0:50–2:00 – Live negotiation.** Transcript scrolling. Narrate: R1 everyone asks for Standard; Commander shows the numeric
conflict; R2 Restricted still too much; R3 optimizer drafts a plan, validator says FAIL (no commitments), the sacrificing
department REFUSES, commitments arrive from two different agents, plan v2 replaces v1 and clears votes; R4 validator PASS,
four ACCEPT votes, Commander approves. Open **Mode Board** and **Validation**.

**2:00–3:15 – Crisis.** Inject an event from the dashboard (e.g. Power -4, or a new one you invent). Show: old plan marked
STALE, commitment review, Crisis Override notice, at least two new rounds, fresh votes, new approved plan.
Optionally inject something impossible (e.g. Power 30%) to show **INFEASIBLE** with the exact extra resource needed.

**3:15–3:45 – Evidence.** Click Export buttons; show `final_allocation.json` and the persistent **All scenarios** transcript.

**3:45–4:00 – Close.** Mention fallback labelling, tests, and known limitations from the README.
